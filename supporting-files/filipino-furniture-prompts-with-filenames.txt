GENERATION AND EXPORT NOTES
Set the output dimensions in the generator or export tool; including pixel dimensions only in prompt text does not guarantee the delivered pixel size. When the tool cannot output an exact size, generate at a sufficiently large resolution with the closest supported aspect ratio, then crop and resize to the listed dimensions without stretching.

Use an approved first image as a visual reference for later images when the generator supports reference inputs. Matching text alone does not guarantee identical furniture across separately generated images.

Preview the images on desktop and mobile. Cover cropping can remove portions of a landscape image on a narrow screen. Keep the main focal furniture central and allow secondary surroundings to be cropped.

Do not bake the HTML's size-map labels, site branding, navigation, or captions into the image. The website supplies its own overlays.

An AI-generated project visualization should be identified as a concept rather than used as evidence of a completed installation.
